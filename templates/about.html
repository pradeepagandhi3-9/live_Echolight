About Smart Vision Assistant

We're on a mission to make the visual world accessible to everyone through the power of artificial intelligence. Our assistive technology helps visually impaired individuals understand their surroundings, read text, and navigate with confidence.

Our Mission

To break down visual barriers and create an inclusive world where everyone, regardless of visual ability, can independently interact with their environment. We believe technology should empower, not limit.

Our Vision

A future where visual assistance is seamlessly integrated into daily life, making accessibility universal and natural. We envision a world where no one is left behind in the digital age.

Core Features

OCR Text Extraction

Extract printed text from images, documents, labels, and signs. Read books, menus, and notices with high accuracy using Tesseract OCR technology.

Live Object Detection

Real-time object recognition using YOLOv8. Identify people, furniture, obstacles, and everyday items to navigate safely and independently.

Scene Captioning

AI-powered image descriptions that provide rich context about the environment, helping users understand complex visual scenes in natural language.

Voice Feedback

Hands-free operation with natural voice narration. Receive audio descriptions of your surroundings and extracted text without needing to look at a screen.
